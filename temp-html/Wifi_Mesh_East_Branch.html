Tips for a smooth connection让连接更顺畅的小贴士

Stay on one network只连一个网络Don't switch between networks. Your phone moves between the Wi-Fi boxes by itself.不要在网络之间来回切换。手机会自己在各个 Wi-Fi 设备之间切换。
Big downloads after sessions大文件下载请在课后During events, 30+ people share the connection. Save phone updates and large downloads for later.活动期间 30 多人共用网络。手机更新和大文件下载请留到之后。
Weak signal? Move a little信号弱？挪一挪On the lawn, move toward the house. At the Bagua platform or pyramid, step into the open or toward the white Wi-Fi box. Trees block Wi-Fi.在草坪上，往房子方向走。在八卦平台或金字塔，走到空旷处或朝白色 Wi-Fi 设备走。树木会挡住 Wi-Fi。
Video calls work best indoors视频通话在室内最好The house has the strongest signal. For an important call, stay inside or near the house.房子里信号最强。重要通话请在室内或房子附近进行。

If something isn't working遇到问题怎么办

I can't connect, or the password is refused连不上，或密码被拒绝
  1. Check the network name: it should be EastBranch Wifi.检查网络名称：应为 EastBranch Wifi。
  2. Type the password again. It is case-sensitive.重新输入密码。区分大小写。
  3. If you were here before, "forget" the network in Wi-Fi settings, then join again.如果之前来过，请在 Wi-Fi 设置里"忽略"该网络，再用新密码重新连接。
Connected, but nothing loads已连接，但打不开网页
  1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again on your phone.把手机 Wi-Fi 关掉再打开。
  2. Still stuck? Turn airplane mode on for 10 seconds, then off.还不行？打开飞行模式 10 秒，再关闭。
  3. If everyone nearby has the same problem, tell your host. The internet line may be down.如果周围的人都有同样的问题，请告诉主人。可能是网络线路断了。
It's slow or keeps dropping很慢或经常断线
  1. Move toward the house or the nearest white Wi-Fi box, out from under trees.往房子或最近的白色 Wi-Fi 设备方向走，离开树下。
  2. Close apps that are downloading or backing up photos.关闭正在下载或备份照片的应用。
  3. On the trails between areas the signal can drop. It comes back when you reach the next area.在区域之间的小路上信号可能会断，到了下一个区域就会恢复。

Please leave the Wi-Fi boxes alone请不要动 Wi-Fi 设备

  • The two low, round white Deco units inside the house and the white cylinders on the garage, the Bagua platform and the pyramid are the Wi-Fi. Please don't unplug, move or cover them.屋内的两台低矮圆形白色 Deco，以及车库、八卦平台和金字塔上的白色圆柱，就是 Wi-Fi 设备。请不要拔掉、移动或遮盖它们。
  • A green light means it's working. If you see a red light, let your host know.绿灯表示工作正常。如果看到红灯，请告诉主人。

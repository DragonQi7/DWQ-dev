Rules for Live Trading

Read these before entering the first trade of the session. If one rule is broken, stop and reset.

01

DON'T SCALP TRADE

Do not jump in and out because price is moving fast. No random clicks, boredom trades, revenge trades, or “just one quick scalp.” Wait for a setup you can clearly explain.

Before Entry
Ask: “What is the setup?”
If the answer is unclear, do not trade.
02

CHECK THE MARKET PULSE BEFORE TRADING

Know what the market is doing first. Is it trending, stretched, ranging, breaking out, rejecting a level, or sitting in the middle of nowhere? The trade should fit the current market condition.

Market Context
Know the direction, location, momentum and nearby levels before risking money.
03

FOLLOW THE DOUBLE TOP / DOUBLE BOTTOM — THEN GET OUT

When the setup is a double top or double bottom, trade the pattern you identified. Respect the neckline, entry, stop and intended exit. Do not turn a completed pattern trade into an unplanned hold.

Pattern Discipline
Trade the structure.
Take the planned exit.
04

PUT LIMITS / STOPS ON EVERY TRADE

Every live position must have defined protection and an exit plan. The only exception is when the trade is already being managed by an active trailing stop.

Non-Negotiable
No naked risk.
Stop / target, or active trailing stop.

×

×

×

×

×

Why not just one box? 为什么不能只用一个？

The box is rated for 2,800 sq ft. That sounds huge, but square feet measure an area, not a distance. A 2,800 sq ft circle only reaches about 30 ft out from the box in every direction. Your path from the house to the pyramid is 200 ft.

Three things that shorten the reach even more

Walls and trees

The coverage number is measured in open air. Your router is inside the house, and the path up the hill runs through thick trees.

Your phone is weaker

A phone's antenna is much weaker than the box's. The box may reach your phone, but your phone's signal also has to make it back.

Boxes reach a bit further

Boxes talk to each other further than 30 ft, which is why 60–70 ft gaps work. At 200 ft through trees, one box cannot hold a solid link.

Even if the rating were 28,000 sq ft, it would reach only about 95 ft, less than halfway to the pyramid.

What if the pyramid is higher up? 金字塔地势更高怎么办？

Usually that is fine. The height adds very little distance. What matters is whether anything blocks the straight line between the platform box and the pyramid box.

+30 ftExample: pyramid 30 ft higher

Very steep is the only other problem

These boxes send their signal mostly sideways, like a flat ring. If the pyramid sits almost straight above the platform (about 45° or steeper), the platform box would point underneath it. A normal hillside slope is fine.

Simple test before you buy

Stand at the pyramid and look toward the platform. If you can see where Box C will go, even through a few branches, the link should work. If the hill itself hides the platform, plan a relay box at the high point in between.

30 devices: BE63 or BE25 as the main unit? 30台设备：主设备选BE63还是BE25？

Decision made: 2 × Deco BE25 indoors and 3 × Deco 7 Outdoor BE5000 outdoors were bought and delivered. This tab is the research behind that choice.

Short answer: both can handle 30 devices. TP-Link rates the BE25 for over 150 devices and the BE63 for up to 200. The BE63 handles a heavy load better because it has a third radio lane (6 GHz), so busy devices spread out instead of queuing on one lane.

1
Think of each Wi-Fi band as a lane on a road. Every device in the house has to share a lane. The more lanes, the less waiting.
2
The BE25 has 2 lanes, the BE63 has 3. The BE63's extra 6 GHz lane is used by newer phones and laptops (for example iPhone 15 Pro and later, recent Wi-Fi 6E/7 laptops).
3
The main Deco also carries the outdoor traffic. Everything from the garage, Bagua platform and pyramid comes back to the main Deco over its 5 GHz lane (orange dots below), because the outdoor boxes have no 6 GHz.

Which devices use which lane? 哪些设备走哪条车道？

Devices pick a lane on their own, and the Deco nudges each one to the best lane it supports. You don't set anything. Each lane is a trade-off between speed and reach.

2.4 GHzLongest reach, slowestSmart plugs, speakers, cameras, doorbells and other smart-home gadgets. Almost all of them only have 2.4 GHz. Phones also drop to it when they are far from a Deco.
5 GHzFast, good reach: the main laneNearly every phone, tablet, laptop and TV made in the last 10 years. The outdoor boxes also use it to link back to the main Deco.
6 GHzFastest, shortest reachOnly newer devices with "Wi-Fi 6E" or "Wi-Fi 7" in their specs, such as iPhone 15 Pro and later. Walls block it most, so it works best in the same room as the Deco. Only the BE63 has it.
Do all devices use 6 GHz?

No, only newer ones

Older phones, most laptops more than a few years old, TVs, streaming sticks and every smart-home gadget can't see the 6 GHz lane at all. They keep using 2.4 and 5 GHz on either Deco.

What if none of yours use 6 GHz?

Then the BE63's extra lane sits empty

Everything shares 5 GHz, just like on the BE25, so the two perform about the same. Choose "None" under "Devices that can use 6 GHz" in the simulation below to see it. In that case the BE25 saves you $100.

How to check a device

Look for "Wi-Fi 6E" or "Wi-Fi 7"

Can an indoor Deco connect to the garage box? 室内 Deco 能连车库设备吗？

Yes, easily. Every Deco model links to every other Deco model, indoor or outdoor. The indoor main unit and the outdoor garage box join the same network automatically, the same way two indoor Decos would.

Why it works

60 ft, one wall

The main Deco sits at the X, the driveway end of the house, which faces the garage. The signal crosses about 60 ft and one outside wall. That is a short, easy link.

Tip

Put it near a window facing the garage

Place the main Deco up high, near a window or outside wall on the garage side, not in a cabinet or on the floor. Check the garage box's signal in the Deco app before mounting it.

Trade-off

Garage side vs lawn side

Moving the main Deco toward the lawn helps the lawn but makes the garage link longer. Keep it within about 60 ft of the garage box. Device 2 at the deck end covers the lawn, so device 1 can stay by the Fios router.

Pick your setup 选择连接方式

Recommended

Keep the Fios router, Deco in Access Point mode

The Fios router keeps running the internet. The Decos only add Wi-Fi. This is the right choice if you have Fios TV boxes, because they rely on the Fios router.

Trade-off: in Access Point mode, Deco's parental controls, antivirus and port forwarding are turned off. Use the Fios router's versions instead.

Internet only, advanced

Replace the Fios router

If you have Fios internet only (no Fios TV), the main Deco can plug straight into the Verizon box (the ONT) and act as the router. If your ONT connects by coax instead of a network cable, Verizon may need to turn on the ONT's Ethernet port first.
